# Limitations and Responsible Use

## Evidence limits

- T1 events are controlled/synthetic rather than historical enterprise updates.
- Only 300 evolution events and 60 TEST cases are available.
- FiQA is finance-domain; transfer to other enterprises is untested.
- MiniLM is generic rather than finance-specialized.
- COEXIST remains error-prone and its real-world boundary with NEW/MERGE is subjective.
- SPLIT overlaps with segmentation; three cases per learned/oracle system used a safe no-fabrication fallback.
- Retrieval query coverage and relevance depend on FiQA source provenance.
- Target resolution is distinct from action classification.
- Metric differences do not automatically imply statistical or external significance.

## Operational risks

Lifecycle errors could hide current information, preserve obsolete material, or combine incompatible claims. Use confidence as a review signal only. Require human approval, access controls, rollback, and audit trails. ARCHIVE means inactive retrieval status, never authorization for autonomous physical deletion.

## Responsible claims

This is an academic prototype, not financial advice or a production system. It does not eliminate hallucinations, validate enterprise governance, prove legal novelty, or demonstrate quantum advantage. No quantum hardware or simulator is used. Broader longitudinal enterprise validation and human relevance judgments are required.
