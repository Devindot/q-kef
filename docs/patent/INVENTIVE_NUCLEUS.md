# Proposed Technical Nucleus Under Investigation

The narrow engineering combination is:

1. obtain calibrated lifecycle hypotheses for incoming knowledge;
2. instantiate a lifecycle-specific graph/index `TransitionPlan` for each plausible action;
3. overlay each plan on the same committed epoch without changing live state;
4. execute deterministic witness-query retrieval against each candidate view;
5. compute obsolete exposure, current-evidence miss, churn, and consistency/lineage/temporal/authority checks;
6. quarantine unless one calibrated feasible action is uniquely safe;
7. publish graph and retrieval eligibility under one transition/epoch identity;
8. retain a canonical certificate and compact exact rollback delta.

None of prediction, graph rewrite, shadow evaluation, versioning, synchronization, conformal prediction, or rollback is asserted as independently new. The research question is whether their specified dependency and order produce measurable prevention of unsafe retrieval-state publication. Legal novelty and inventive step are not assessed here.
