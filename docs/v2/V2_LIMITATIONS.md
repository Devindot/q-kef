# Q-KEF v2 Limitations

- The 2,400-event confirmatory benchmark is balanced and ancestry-isolated, but it is controlled synthetic evidence rather than a naturally occurring longitudinal enterprise corpus.
- Automated integrity checks passed all 2,400 events. The stratified 120-row human audit pack is prepared, but independent review and signed adjudication remain pending.
- Dense retrieval outperformed the locked hybrid on TEST (Recall@5 0.8958 versus 0.8875); the proposed hybrid-retrieval improvement was not supported.
- The conventional B0 model led the confirmatory lifecycle comparison (macro-F1 0.8791). Q-full scored 0.8708 and hierarchical Q-full scored 0.8121, so neither the Q-feature nor hierarchical superiority hypotheses were supported against B0.
- Q-full exceeded the matched non-Q control by 0.0191 macro-F1, but this directional difference alone does not establish a statistically reliable Q-specific benefit.
- Mondrian conformal coverage was 0.7792 against a nominal 0.90 target, including 0.0 coverage for ARCHIVE. The calibrated gate therefore failed its coverage objective and must not be represented as production-calibrated uncertainty control.
- Selective execution achieved 0.9466 precision on 281 committed transitions, while quarantining 199 of 480 cases (rate 0.4146). The precision result must be interpreted jointly with that abstention rate.
- Content-derived witnesses are deterministic lexical probes, not observed production workloads. Zero mean current-evidence miss on this benchmark does not prove semantic retrieval safety in open-world use.
- Atomic publication is implemented in a local in-memory runtime. It is not a distributed transaction protocol and does not address network partitions, multi-writer concurrency, or durable recovery across processes.
- Authority and bitemporal fields are supported, but the authority hierarchy and temporal mutations are synthetic rather than derived from audited operational histories.
- Local update latency was approximately 2.98 seconds p50 and 4.22 seconds p95. These measurements are machine- and benchmark-specific and do not establish production-scale performance.
- The experiment establishes neither quantum computational advantage nor production readiness. Its prior-art and invention materials are research aids, not legal advice or a patentability opinion.
